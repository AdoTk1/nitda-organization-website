About NITDA

The National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) is a Federal Government agency responsible for developing and regulating Information Technology in Nigeria.

Our History

The National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) was established in April 2001 to implement Nigeria's Information Technology Policy and coordinate the development of information technology in the country.

The Agency was established to create a framework for the planning, research, development, standardization, application, coordination, monitoring, evaluation and regulation of Information Technology practices and activities in Nigeria.

Over the years, NITDA has played an important role in promoting digital transformation, technological innovation and the development of Nigeria's digital economy.

Our Mission

To drive digital transformation through innovation, accessibility and enabling regulations, fostering a digitally empowered nation.

Our Vision

To make Nigeria a digitally empowered nation, fostering inclusive economic growth through technological innovation.
